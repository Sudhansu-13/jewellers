Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers regarding our gold quality, making charges, custom orders, and exchange terms.

Are all gold items at Pashupatinath Jewellers hallmark certified?
Yes, absolutely. Every piece of gold jewellery we craft and sell is 100% genuine 24K (99.9% Chhapawal) or 22K (91.6% Tejabi) hallmarked and tested under strict standards. We provide verified receipts and authenticity assurance with every single purchase.
Can I customize jewellery with my own design or photo?
Yes! We specialize in custom bespoke jewellery. Simply share your reference photos or sketches with our master craftsmen either by visiting our Bardaghat showroom or calling our lines (9857054299). We can adjust the design to match your exact preferred gold weight and budget.
What is your old gold exchange policy?
We offer 100% full market value for genuine gold purity with transparent digital scale weight verification. We do not make arbitrary melting deductions. You can exchange your old or broken gold ornaments for any brand-new design instantly.
What are your showroom opening hours in Bardaghat?
Our showroom in Bardaghat-4, Nawalparasi is open from Sunday to Friday between 10:00 AM to 7:30 PM. For emergency appointments, wedding consultations, or urgent orders, you can reach Proprietor Susil Sunar directly at 9857054299 / 9819455299.
How are making charges (Jyala / Mazdoori) calculated?
Our making charges are among the most competitive and transparent in Nawalparasi and Lumbini province. Charges depend on the intricacy of the handmade filigree work, starting from reasonable standard rates with zero hidden overheads.
